# Authors & credits

## STOS BASIC (1987–1988, Atari ST)

- **François Lionet** — language design and 68000 implementation: `BASIC.S`,
  `FLOAT.S`/`DFLOAT.S`, `SPRITES.S`, `FENETRE.S`, the picture compactor
  (`COMPACT.S`) and the sprite designer (`SPRITE.ASC`). Hand-written on the soft
  keyboard of an Atari 520 ST.
- **C. Sotiropoulos** — co-author of the original STOS BASIC, as credited on the
  boot screen: *"By F. Lionet & C. Sotiropoulos"* (`BASIC.S`).
- Published by **Jawx / Mandarin** (1988).

## STAS — STOS ASCII System (2026)

- **François Lionet** — direction, faithful-port decisions, and verification of
  every semantic choice against the 1987–88 68000 sources.
- Written with **AI assistance** (Zed + a large language model): the AI reads the
  original sources, proposes, and drafts; the human decides, corrects, and
  validates. The *authored* contribution is the human one — the sources, the
  decisions, the corrections — as recorded day by day in the git history and in
  `JOURNAL.md`.
